# Campa range references

Verified on 17 October 2026. Product listings verify a range or packaging reference, not local inventory or a promise of delivery. The presentation is a curated range, not an exhaustive catalogue. Retailer images are genuine product photographs; pack designs can vary by market and batch.

## Official range and imagery

- [Official Campa website](https://campabeverages.com/) names Cola, Orange, Lemon, PowerUp strong cola, and Cricket lemon electrolyte refreshment.
- Existing `campa-cola.png`, `campa-orange.png`, and `campa-lemon.png` are the official product compositions used in the original prototype. All three have can and bottle format references in [Metro's retailer catalogue](https://www.metro.co.in/pdf/trader-catalogue/winter-catalogue/Bangalore1.pdf). Their immersive campaign sections retain the previously generated can-and-bottle imagery.
- PowerUp official bottle composition: [official CDN asset](https://campabeveragesadmin.ril.com/uploads/Powerup_Big_Bottle_Hover_78828cdbbe_b8334a780f.png), saved as `public/images/range/powerup-cola.png`.
- Cricket official bottle composition: [official CDN asset](https://campabeveragesadmin.ril.com/uploads/Campa_cricket_Bottle_238881cf10.png), saved as `public/images/range/cricket.png`.
